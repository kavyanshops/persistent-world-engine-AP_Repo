Normalization path

0NF

One wide order record with repeating item and payment groups and a multi-valued cuisines field.

1NF

Atomic values. Repeating groups become rows: order_items, payments, one review per order.

2NF

Partial dependencies removed. Item facts live in menu_items; order_items keeps only quantity and sale price.

3NF

Transitive dependencies removed: city → locality → restaurant → order. Natural keys stay UNIQUE.
